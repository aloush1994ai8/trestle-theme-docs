Theme documentation

Getting started

  1. Install or upload the Trestle theme to Shopify.
  2. Keep the theme unpublished while configuring navigation, collections, products and brand settings.
  3. Open Online Store → Themes → Customize.
  4. Configure global colors, typography, layout width, buttons and motion preferences in Theme settings.
  5. Build the home page using Trestle sections, then verify product, collection, search, cart, blog, article, contact and password templates.

Recommended content setup

  • Use clear collection names and high-quality 1:1 or editorial images.
  • Keep product titles concise and product descriptions factual.
  • Use Shopify-native filters, variants, selling plans, pickup availability and product recommendations where relevant.
  • Use image focal points to protect important subjects across responsive crops.

Sections and blocks

Trestle includes flexible home-page and editorial sections, product blocks, app blocks and Custom Liquid support. Section defaults are intentionally neutral so merchants can configure content without inheriting fabricated store claims.

Product pages

Trestle supports rich product media, variant-linked media, swatches, selling plans, pickup availability, accelerated checkout, Shop Pay payment terms, recommendations, complementary products and no-JavaScript purchasing.

Collections and search

Collection and search templates support Shopify-native faceted filtering and sorting. Product cards include price, compare-at pricing, unit pricing where applicable, media fallbacks and accessible quick-add behavior.

Accessibility

  • Keyboard-visible focus states are included.
  • Navigation and product controls expose state with ARIA semantics.
  • Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
  • Continuous marquee motion is disabled in shipped defaults.
  • Dialog and age-verification focus behavior is designed for keyboard users.

Performance

Use correctly sized images, avoid unnecessary app scripts, keep above-the-fold media optimized, and test the home, collection and product templates on desktop and mobile before publishing.

Apps and custom content

Use Shopify app blocks where available. Trestle also includes Custom Liquid for merchant-authored markup. Third-party app behavior and performance remain the responsibility of the installed app.

Frequently asked questions

Should I publish Trestle before configuring it?

No. Configure and test the theme while it is unpublished, then publish only after navigation, catalog, checkout handoff and responsive QA are complete.

Does Trestle support Shopify app blocks?

Yes. Use app blocks in supported sections instead of hard-coding app snippets whenever the app provides them.

How should I prepare images?

Use high-quality source images with enough resolution for responsive crops. Set Shopify focal points where the subject must remain visible at different aspect ratios.

Can I disable motion?

Trestle respects operating-system reduced-motion preferences. Merchant-configurable motion should remain purposeful and avoid continuous movement that shoppers cannot control.

What should I include in a support request?

Include the store URL, theme version, affected page, browser/device, steps to reproduce, expected behavior, actual behavior and screenshots or recordings when useful.

What is the support response policy?

Support requests should receive an initial response within two business days. Complex issues may require additional investigation after the initial response.
